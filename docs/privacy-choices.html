Your Privacy Choices

您的隐私选择

Data Control

数据控制

BillMind gives you control over your data. Your trips and bills are stored on our backend service so they sync across your devices, encrypted in transit. We use your data only to provide the app — never to sell, advertise, or track you across other apps.

BillMind 让您掌控自己的数据。您的旅行记录和账单存储在我们的后端服务上，以便在您的多台设备间同步，并在传输过程中加密。我们仅使用您的数据来提供应用——绝不出售、用于广告，或在其他应用中追踪您。

AI Processing

AI 处理

AI powers BillMind's recognition and Minds features. When you use them, your input and the relevant bill data are processed through our backend by Google Gemini, after being safety-screened by OpenAI's moderation API. You do not need to provide your own API key. You can:

AI 为 BillMind 的识别功能和 Minds 功能提供支持。当您使用这些功能时，您的输入和相关账单数据会先经 OpenAI 审核 API 进行安全筛查，再通过我们的后端由 Google Gemini 处理。您无需提供自己的 API 密钥。您可以：

  • Use AI features on demand — Or add bills manually whenever you prefer
  • Review before saving — AI suggestions are always yours to confirm or edit
  • Delete anytime — Remove bills, trips, or your whole account at any time
  • 按需使用 AI 功能 — 也可以随时手动添加账单
  • 保存前审核 — AI 的识别结果始终由您确认或编辑
  • 随时删除 — 随时删除账单、旅行日记或整个账户

Camera & Photos

相机和照片

Camera and photo library access is requested only when you choose to add a receipt photo. You can deny these permissions and still use the app by entering bills manually.

仅在您选择添加发票照片时才会请求相机和相册权限。您可以拒绝这些权限，仍然可以手动输入账单使用应用。

Delete Your Data

删除您的数据

You can remove your data from our servers at any time:

您可以随时从我们的服务器中移除您的数据：

  • Delete individual bills or entire trips within the app
  • Delete your account in the app (Settings > Delete Account) to permanently remove your trips and bills from our servers
  • Email cdcupt@gmail.com with any access or deletion request
  • 在应用内删除单条账单或整个旅行日记
  • 在应用内删除账户（设置 > 删除账户），将您的旅行日记和账单从我们的服务器中永久移除
  • 如有任何访问或删除请求，发送电子邮件至 cdcupt@gmail.com

No Ads, No Cross-App Tracking

无广告，无跨应用追踪

BillMind does not sell your data, show ads, or use your data for cross-app tracking or advertising. We use your data only to provide the app.

BillMind 不会出售您的数据、展示广告，也不会将您的数据用于跨应用追踪或广告。我们仅使用您的数据来提供应用。
