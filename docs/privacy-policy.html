2. Data Collection & Storage

二、数据收集与存储

Our Backend Service

BillOwl operates a backend service, hosted in the United States, that stores your account and travel expense data so it is available across your sessions and devices. All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

Data We Store

On our servers we store:

  • Your trips (journals)
  • Your bills — merchant name, amount, date, category, and notes
  • Receipt photos you choose to submit
  • From sign-in: your email address and a stable provider user identifier used to create and identify your account

If you sign in with Apple and use "Hide My Email," the private relay address is not stored as your identity.

Sign-In

You sign in with Google or Apple using OAuth / OpenID Connect. We receive a stable user identifier and an email address from your chosen provider in order to create and access your account.

No User-Provided API Keys

You do not provide your own AI API keys. AI features are powered by our backend using our own credentials.

我们的后端服务

BillOwl 运营一项托管于美国的后端服务，用于存储您的账户和旅行消费数据，以便在您的不同会话和设备之间使用。所有数据在传输过程中均通过 HTTPS/TLS 加密。

我们存储的数据

我们在服务器上存储：

  • 您的旅行日记
  • 您的账单——商户名称、金额、日期、类别和备注
  • 您选择提交的收据照片
  • 来自登录：您的电子邮件地址和一个稳定的服务商用户标识符，用于创建和识别您的账户

如果您使用 Apple 登录并启用"隐藏我的邮箱"，该私密转发地址不会被作为您的身份进行存储。

登录

您可以通过 Google 或 Apple 使用 OAuth / OpenID Connect 登录。我们会从您选择的服务商处获得一个稳定的用户标识符和电子邮件地址，用于创建和访问您的账户。

无需用户自带 API 密钥

您无需提供自己的 AI API 密钥。AI 功能由我们的后端使用我们自己的凭据提供支持。

3. AI Features — Data Sent and Recipients

三、AI 功能 — 发送的数据与接收方

AI is the core of BillOwl. Our backend service uses third-party AI providers to power bill recognition and timeline image generation. We call these providers with our own API credentials — you do not provide your own keys.

AI Providers We Use

  • Google Gemini (Google LLC) — bill recognition and Minds timeline image generation. Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI (OpenAI, Inc.) — content moderation that screens your input for safety. Privacy Policy

How AI Requests Flow

When you use an AI feature, your input is first screened by OpenAI's moderation API for safety. If it passes, the relevant data is sent to Google's Gemini API for processing. These requests are made by our backend using our own credentials.

What Data Is Sent

Bill Recognition: The typed text or receipt photo you submit, plus the relevant bill data, is sent to Google Gemini to extract merchant name, date, amount, currency, category, line items, and notes.

Timeline Generation (Minds): When you generate a Minds timeline image, your trip data — journal name, currency, and a bill timeline summary (merchant names, dates, amounts, and categories) — is sent to Google Gemini along with a prompt requesting an artistic infographic.

Moderation: Each user input is sent to OpenAI's moderation API to screen it for safety before it reaches Gemini.

How AI Providers Use Your Data

Each AI provider processes your data according to its own privacy policy (linked above). We recommend reviewing those policies. We use these providers only to deliver the App's features.

AI 是 BillOwl 的核心。我们的后端服务使用第三方 AI 服务商来实现账单识别和时间线图片生成。我们使用自己的 API 凭据调用这些服务商——您无需提供自己的密钥。

我们使用的 AI 服务商

  • Google Gemini（Google LLC）— 账单识别和 Minds 时间线图片生成。隐私政策
  • OpenAI（OpenAI, Inc.）— 对您的输入进行安全审核。隐私政策

AI 请求的流程

当您使用 AI 功能时，您的输入会首先经过 OpenAI 的审核 API 进行安全筛查。通过后，相关数据会被发送至 Google 的 Gemini API 进行处理。这些请求均由我们的后端使用我们自己的凭据发起。

发送哪些数据

账单识别：您提交的文本或收据照片，以及相关账单数据，将发送至 Google Gemini，用于提取商户名称、日期、金额、币种、分类、明细和备注。

时间线生成（Minds）：当您生成 Minds 时间线图片时，您的旅行数据——日记名称、币种以及账单时间线摘要（商户名称、日期、金额和分类）——将连同请求生成艺术信息图的提示一并发送至 Google Gemini。

内容审核：每条用户输入在到达 Gemini 之前，都会发送至 OpenAI 的审核 API 进行安全筛查。

AI 服务商如何使用您的数据

每个 AI 服务商根据其自身的隐私政策（上方已附链接）处理您的数据。我们建议您查阅这些政策。我们仅使用这些服务商来提供本应用的功能。
