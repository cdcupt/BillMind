FAQ

常见问题

How do I get started?

如何开始使用？

Open the app and sign in with Google or Apple. AI is built in — there's nothing to configure. Then create a trip and start adding bills, either by photographing receipts or entering them manually.

打开应用，使用 Google 或 Apple 登录。AI 已内置——无需任何配置。然后创建一段旅行日记，开始添加账单，可以拍摄收据，也可以手动录入。

How does AI bill recognition work?

AI 账单识别如何工作？

Take a photo of a receipt or select one from your library. The app sends it through our backend to Google Gemini, which extracts the merchant name, amount, date, category, and line items. You can review and edit the results before saving.

拍摄收据照片或从相册选择。应用会通过我们的后端将其发送至 Google Gemini，提取商户名称、金额、日期、类别和明细。保存前您可以检查和编辑识别结果。

Which AI services does BillOwl use?

BillOwl 使用哪些 AI 服务？

  • Google Gemini — bill recognition and Minds image generation
  • OpenAI — content moderation that safety-screens your input
  • Google Gemini — 账单识别和 Minds 图片生成
  • OpenAI — 对您的输入进行安全筛查的内容审核

What is the Minds feature?

什么是 Minds 功能？

Minds generates a sketch-style infographic timeline from your journal's bill data using AI image generation. It creates a visual summary of your trip spending.

Minds 功能使用 AI 图片生成，根据日记中的账单数据创建手绘风格的信息图时间线，直观展示您的旅行消费。

Where is my data stored?

数据存储在哪里？

Your trips, bills, and receipt photos are stored on BillOwl's backend service, hosted in the United States, so they sync across your devices. All data is encrypted in transit. You can delete your account anytime in Settings > Delete Account, which permanently removes your data from our servers. See our Privacy Policy for details.

您的旅行记录、账单和收据照片存储在 BillOwl 托管于美国的后端服务上，以便在您的多台设备间同步。所有数据在传输过程中均经过加密。您可以随时在"设置 > 删除账户"中删除账户，将您的数据从我们的服务器中永久移除。详情请参阅我们的隐私政策。

Which currencies are supported?

支持哪些货币？

BillOwl supports multiple currencies including CNY, USD, EUR, JPY, GBP, KRW, THB, and more. Each journal can have its own base currency.

BillOwl 支持多种货币，包括人民币、美元、欧元、日元、英镑、韩元、泰铢等。每个旅行日记可以设置不同的基础货币。
