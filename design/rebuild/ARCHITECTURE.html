One home: record & review, together

A ground-up rebuild. Journals and Record merge into a single home; the app is re-centered on capture, with a fresh visual direction. Stats and Minds stay (re-scoped); the verified agent core is ported intact.

tabs Record · Stats · Minds · Settings merge Journals + Record → one home depth fresh redesign, agent core ported

The old app made you choose a journal, then open a modal to add a bill. The rebuild collapses that: the home IS the trip, and capture lives at the bottom of it. You see your trip's bills as a timeline and talk to the agent in the same place — no tab-hop, no modal. Journals doesn't disappear; it's absorbed.

1The merge — what "Journals + Record" becomes

The Trip (today's Journal) stays as the data grouping — it's what makes this a travel tracker — but the standalone Journals tab is gone. The Record tab becomes the home and does both jobs:

Browse (was Journals)

  • Active-trip header: cover, name, dates, running total
  • A timeline of that trip's bills, grouped by day
  • A trip switcher in the header (and "new trip")

Capture (was Record)

  • A pinned input bar at the bottom of the same screen
  • Type / photo → the agent's editable bill cards stream inline
  • Confirm → the bill drops into the timeline above

So one scroll surface = your trip + its bills + the way you add more. Tapping a saved bill opens its detail (kept). Switching trips swaps the whole surface.

4Visual direction — "Voyage"

Away from the childish hand-drawn sketch, toward a refined travel-ledger: editorial warmth + the precision a money app needs. Honors your standing preference — light background, near-black text, strong accent, list/timeline navigation.
