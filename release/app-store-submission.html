3 · Store text

Promotional text (≤170)

Just say what you spent — BillOwl's AI reads it, sorts it, and tallies your trip. Capture by text, voice, or a photo of any receipt. Nothing saves until you tap Save.

Description (≤4000)

BillOwl is the travel expense tracker that does the typing for you. Tell it what you spent — in plain words, with your voice, or by snapping a receipt — and its AI agent turns it into a clean, editable bill in seconds. No spreadsheets, no fiddly forms.

Capture any way you like
• Type it: "ramen 2840" or "lunch 500, taxi 200, coffee 80" → one card per expense.
• Say it: tap the mic and speak; BillOwl transcribes and files it.
• Snap it: photograph a receipt and the AI reads the merchant, amount, date, and category.

Smart, but never sneaky
• The AI recognizes as much as it can and asks when something's unclear.
• It never guesses an amount — if a total isn't clear, it asks you.
• Nothing is saved until you tap Save. Every card is fully editable.

See where your money went
• Organize spending into trips, each in its own currency.
• Stats break down totals by trip, category, and day.
• "Minds" turns a trip into a beautiful hand-drawn timeline of your spending.

Yours, across devices
• Sign in with Apple or Google. Your trips sync securely so they're there on every device.
• Delete any bill, any trip, or your whole account anytime.

BillOwl is built for travelers, freelancers, and anyone who wants expense tracking to feel like a quick conversation instead of a chore.

In App Store Connect → App Privacy, declare the following. All data is "Linked to you", used only for "App Functionality", and NOT used for Tracking. Answer NO to "Do you or your third-party partners use data for tracking?"

Do NOT declare: Location, Contacts, Browsing/Search History, Health, Purchases, Diagnostics/Analytics, Advertising data — BillOwl collects none of these. The voice feature transcribes on-device (Apple Speech) and the transcript text follows the "Other User Content" path; the audio itself is not stored.

5 · Age rating questionnaire → 4+

Answer None to every content question (no violence, profanity, sexual content, gambling, drugs, horror, mature/suggestive themes). No to Unrestricted Web Access. The app has no public user-generated-content sharing (the AI "Minds" image is generated from your own private data and stays in your account), so the UGC/moderation question does not gate the rating. Result: 4+. Note for your records: AI-generated content exists (bill recognition + Minds) and every user input is screened by OpenAI's moderation API before reaching the model.

6 · App Review information

Sign-in / demo account

No demo account needed. Put this in "Notes":

BillOwl requires sign-in. Please tap "Sign in with Apple" on the launch screen and use your own Apple ID — this creates a fresh account instantly (no credentials required). Then on the Record tab, type for example "ramen 2840" and tap the send arrow: the AI returns an editable bill card; tap "Save to trip" to save it. The app needs network access — AI recognition runs on our US-hosted backend, and every input is screened by content moderation. To remove the account: Settings → Delete Account.
